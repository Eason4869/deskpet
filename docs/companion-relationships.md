# Companion home

Open the avatar menu and choose **View companion home**. The page opens in the
main conversation window and participates in Back/Forward navigation. Returning
to a conversation keeps its existing draft and session. General application
preferences keep their existing host on each platform.

The page shows the selected companion's original portrait, introduction, saved
relationship stage, cumulative points, today's points, and earned milestones.
Active days count days with actual activity; days since meeting count the
inclusive calendar span from the first recorded meeting. They can differ.
Unlocked welcomes use the companion pack's supported localized content, with
the application's stage welcome as a fallback.

Stages begin at 0, 80, 240, 480, 760, and 1000 points. A visit earns 2 points
(up to 2 a day), an interaction earns 1 (up to 6 a day, at least 20 seconds
apart), a completed conversation earns 2 (up to 8 a day), and successful work
earns 4 (up to 8 a day). The combined limit is 20 points per day. At 1000,
the page displays the maximum stage without a next-stage target.

The inline relationship settings apply globally. Growth, feedback, and AI stage
context can be switched independently. Settings controls show committed values;
turning growth off preserves existing records. If saving fails, the previous
setting remains visible and Retry retains the requested change.

If the relationship file cannot be read, the page keeps the last successfully
read view for that companion, when available, and shows a warning with Retry.
An unreadable file is preserved. An unknown saved state is never displayed as a
new zero-point relationship. Pending activity saves are retried through the
shared runtime with their original event identifiers.

Milestone celebration dialogs and card export are separate from this page.
